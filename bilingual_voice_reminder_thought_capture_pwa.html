Add SunoYad to Phone Home Screen

Why install as a PWA?

Runs just like a native iPhone or Android app without needing the App Store. Works offline and stays on your home screen for quick voice triggers!

For iPhone / Safari:

  1. Open this web app link in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" .
  4. Tap Add in the top right. SunoYad is now on your home screen!

For Android / Chrome:

  1. Open this web app link in Google Chrome on your Android device.
  2. Tap the three dots menu in the top right corner.
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  4. Confirm the prompt. You can now launch it instantly like any other app!
